Frequently asked questions

Do you run wires or cables?

No. We never run wires or cables. Outdoor cameras run on our solar-powered connected system, and indoor setups use a normal AC outlet.

How high will you install a camera?

We install no higher than 10 feet so the work is safe and the camera stays easy to reach. If your best spot is higher, we will suggest a safer location.

What is the Visit Guarantee?

If we can’t complete your task in the time set for your visit, you pay nothing for that visit. The time is written on your quote first. Some exceptions apply, such as no access to the home. See Services.

Do the police get called automatically?

No. Cameras let you see your property and receive notifications on your phone. Foxtrot does not provide alarm monitoring or emergency dispatch. In an emergency, call 911.

Do I have to pay a monthly fee to use the cameras?

Our monthly plan is a fixed-term payment for equipment, installation, and a monthly service call. Separately, the camera app maker may offer optional paid cloud features. Those are your choice.

What does the monthly service call cover?

One visit each month while your contract is active, for help with your Foxtrot-installed equipment and app. It does not carry over to later months. Parts, extra work, and unrelated repairs are quoted first.

What happens when my contract ends?

Monthly payments and monthly service calls stop. The equipment is yours as stated in your agreement. Extra help is available at our regular rates.

I’m not good with phones. Can you help?

Yes. We set up the app with you and walk you through it in person. With your permission, a family member may join.

What is a Digital Safety Checkup?

We look at your devices, internet connection, web browser, and passwords, then give you a simple safety score and a plain-language plan. We can make the fixes with you.

Do you need my passwords?

Never by text or email. You enter your own passwords yourself. We do not keep them.

Can I rent or have a business?

Renters need written permission from the property owner. Ask us about businesses.

What areas do you serve?

Homes within 60 miles of [BASE CITY/ZIP]. Beyond 60 miles, a mileage-based travel fee at the current IRS standard mileage rate applies. Call to confirm your address.
